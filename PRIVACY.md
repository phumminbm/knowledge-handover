# Privacy

knowledge-handover has no server, no account and no analytics. This page explains where your handover data goes.

## The skill

The skill is a set of instructions and scripts that run inside the AI app you choose (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, or another). What you type and the images you share are processed by that app under **its** privacy terms and your organisation's agreement with it. This project receives nothing.

The skill tells the AI to:
- never record passwords, keys or one-time codes, and to tell you to change any secret you paste;
- point to the official record (CRM, patient system, register) instead of copying personal contact details, health information, salaries or prices;
- ask you to crop or cover personal data in screenshots and photos before they go into a Pack.

## The Pack Builder and START-HERE.html

Both are single HTML files that run entirely in your browser. They make no network requests: fonts and images are embedded, nothing is uploaded, and nothing is stored outside your browser. The hosted Pack Builder on GitHub Pages is the same file; GitHub serves it, so GitHub sees that the page was requested, but not what you paste into it.

## Knowledge Packs

A Pack contains whatever the giver shared. Treat it as an internal document: store and send it only through channels your organisation approves, such as its shared drive, rather than personal email or public links.
